# Admission guidelines for international students (2026–2027)

All international students must apply through the portal of the Ministry of Education and Science of the Kyrgyz Republic (Edugate portal).

## Admission timeline (Summer intake)
- Admission period: Mid-July – End of August.
- Document submission deadline: September 1, 2026.
- Recommended latest arrival date in Bishkek: September 15, 2026.

## Eligibility criteria
- Minimum average score of 65% in secondary/high school education.
- Successful completion of the university entrance examination.
- English language proficiency assessment upon arrival.

Applicants with international English certificates may submit one of: TOEFL — minimum 80; IELTS — minimum 5.5; Duolingo English Test — minimum 100.

## Required documents
- Copy of passport.
- High school certificate/diploma with academic transcript.
- Six passport-size photographs (3×4 cm).
- Notarized Russian translations of all educational and identification documents.

Obtained after arrival in Kyrgyzstan: medical insurance (Foms.kg); registration card from the Public Service Center; Individual Identification Number (IIN/PIN). IIN/PIN registration address: AYU GRAND, Chokan Valikhanov Street 2, Building 8, 1st Floor, Bishkek.

## Application process
1. Review academic programs on the official university website and select a department.
2. Register and submit required documents through the Ministry of Education and Science (Edugate) portal: passport photo, high school certificate, academic transcript, available translations.
3. After document review, eligible applicants receive a link to the university entrance examination.
4. Applicants who pass the exam and meet requirements receive admission confirmation and an official admission letter.
5. Complete the online application form, upload all documents and submit.

All notarized translations must be completed in Kyrgyzstan; the university provides guidance upon arrival.

## Visa and enrollment
- Visa support documents are provided only after successful admission.
- Upon arrival, applicants must submit all original documents.
- To complete enrollment, students pay 50% of the annual tuition fee upon arrival.

## Scholarships and accommodation
- At present, the university does not offer scholarships for international applicants.
- The university does not currently operate its own dormitory. Private hostels, student residences and rental apartments are available near the university and across Bishkek; staff may assist newly admitted students in finding accommodation.
